Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

What We Collect

JobPilot AI collects the following data to provide its services:

  • Account information: Email address and display name (for authentication via Firebase).
  • Application preferences: Job search terms, profile details, and Q&A answers you configure in the extension.
  • Usage statistics: Number of applications sent, messages sent, and session counts (for plan limit enforcement).

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT store your LinkedIn password on any server. It is stored locally in your browser only.
  • We do NOT read or store your LinkedIn messages, connections, or profile data.
  • We do NOT sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do NOT track your browsing activity outside of LinkedIn.

Data Storage

Your settings are stored in two places:

  • Locally: In Chrome's extension storage on your device (chrome.storage.local).
  • Cloud: In Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud) for sync across devices. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

LinkedIn Credentials

If you choose to save your LinkedIn email and password for auto-login, they are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are never uploaded to any server.

Payment Information

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. We do not store your card details, UPI ID, or bank information. Cashfree handles all payment data securely under PCI-DSS compliance.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • Use the "Clear All Data" button in the extension's Settings tab.
  • Or email us at mukul798348@gmail.com to request account deletion.

Permissions Used

  • storage: To save your preferences locally.
  • activeTab / tabs: To interact with LinkedIn pages for auto-applying.
  • notifications: To alert you about bot status.
  • identity: For Google sign-in authentication.
